Adarsh Savana vs Godrej Varanya: the short answer

Choose Adarsh Savana Phase 3 if you want:

  • Published plot sizes and a clubhouse size from the developer
  • A large layout with earlier phases you can visit
  • A project that already shows a RERA number (though you must check which phase it covers)

Consider a Godrej plot in Doddaballapur if you want:

  • The Godrej brand name
  • A newer launch
  • A location on the Doddaballapur side of North Bengaluru

Before either, read the next section. The Godrej side of this comparison has a big question mark.

A warning about the "Godrej Varanya" name

The name "Godrej Varanya, Bangalore" appears on marketing websites. We found no K-RERA record under that name. We also found no government or major-news report of it.

Here is what we can confirm about Godrej plots in the area:

  • In July 2025, Business Standard reported that Godrej Properties bought 48 acres in Doddaballapur for plots.
  • The report put the saleable area at 1.1 million sq ft.
  • It did not name the project.

Marketing sites use more than one name for Godrej plots in Doddaballapur. We cannot tell whether these refer to the same project, two projects, or a naming mix-up.

What this means for you: wait before you book a "Godrej Varanya" plot in Bangalore. First, find the project on the K-RERA portal under Godrej's name. Ask the sales desk for the exact project name, survey numbers and RERA number.

In the rest of this guide, "Godrej Varanya" means the Godrej plot project on the Doddaballapur land, whatever its final name. Our RERA checks in the Savana review work for both projects.

Location: Savana vs Doddaballapur

Both projects sit in North Bengaluru. But they sit on different sides, and that shapes daily life.

Adarsh Savana: the airport side

Adarsh Developers places Savana in Yelahanka Extension, near Kempegowda International Airport. It markets the layout as plots near Devanahalli. The main route to the city is NH 44 via Yelahanka and Hebbal.

Nearby anchors include:

  • Kempegowda International Airport
  • Stonehill International School and the Padukone-Dravid Centre, each about a 5-minute drive, per Adarsh

Adarsh gives no km distance to the airport. Map it yourself from the site gate.

See our Devanahalli location guide for more.

Godrej: the Doddaballapur side

Business Standard linked the Godrej Doddaballapur land to good airport links and the Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR). It also named Nandi Hills as a nearby draw. The report gave no distances.

The STRR's Doddaballapur–Hoskote section opened for tolls in November 2023, per Deccan Herald. That road links Doddaballapur with the airport side of the city.

Which location suits you?

  • You work at the airport: Savana's side may be closer. Check the drive.
  • You work in Doddaballapur: a Doddaballapur plot may cut your commute.
  • You work in central Bengaluru: both are long drives. Test each at peak hour.
  • You work in East or South Bengaluru: both are hard daily commutes.

This is the biggest gap between the two.

Adarsh also claims BIAAPA approval and A-khata. Our Adarsh Savana Phase 3 review explains how to check.

Godrej Varanya

We found no RERA number for a Godrej Varanya project in Bangalore as of September 2026.

Under the RERA Act, plotted projects of 500 sq m or more must register with the state regulator, per PIB. So be wary of any booking offer that comes before a RERA number.

Your legal checklist for either project

  1. Find the project on K-RERA under the builder's name.
  2. Match the survey numbers to your plot.
  3. Get the DC conversion order and the layout approval.
  4. Get the khata extract for your plot.
  5. Have your own lawyer check a 30-year title.

Our plot document checklist covers each paper. For khata types, read our A-khata vs B-khata guide.

Developer track record

Adarsh Developers says it has more than 32 years of experience in real estate. It calls Savana its first plotted development. Earlier Savana phases give you a place to test its work on the ground.

Godrej Properties bought its Doddaballapur land in 2025, per Business Standard. So there is no older Godrej phase at that site to inspect.

In both cases, check the K-RERA portal for complaints against the builder's other projects. A brand name helps, but it does not replace your own checks.

Questions to ask both sales teams

Put the same questions to each builder. Then compare the answers side by side. Ask for each answer in writing.

  1. What is the exact project name and RERA number?
  2. What are the survey numbers for my plot?
  3. What is the rate per sq ft, and what premiums apply?
  4. What builder charges will I pay on top?
  5. Which charges carry GST?
  6. What is the handover date in the RERA record?
  7. What are the building rules for my plot?
  8. Which banks have approved the project?
  9. What is the refund if I cancel?

If one team cannot answer the first question, stop there. A plot without a clear RERA record is not ready to buy.

Walk both sites on the same day if you can. Drive each route at peak hour. That one trip will tell you more about the commute than any listing. Our plot loan guide helps with question 8.

Our verdict

For most plot buyers today, Adarsh Savana Phase 3 is the easier project to evaluate. It has published sizes, a known layout, older phases to visit and a RERA number to search. Its RERA gap is a real flaw, but a search on the K-RERA portal can settle it.

Godrej Varanya is not ready for a fair comparison yet. We could not confirm the name, the price or a RERA number. If Godrej launches its Doddaballapur plot project with full RERA details, it may be worth a look. Until then, treat any "Varanya" offer with care.
