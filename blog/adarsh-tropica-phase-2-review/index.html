About the builder

Adarsh Developers started in Bengaluru in 1988, per its own site. Its registered office is at Langford Gardens in Richmond Town. The group works in housing, offices and hotels.

Adarsh Developers lists 33-plus completed projects and more than 21 ongoing ones. We have not checked those counts against a full list.

How Phase 2 fits the wider Tropica project

Tropica is a two-phase project, and Adarsh lists a separate RERA number for each. Phase 1's number carries the date digits "111022", which points to a 2022 registration. Phase 2's number points to 2025.

That split explains some of the noise online. Some third-party pages seem to mix data from both phases. Tower counts, unit counts and hand-over dates vary from page to page.

Why should you care?

  • Timeline. Phase 1 should hand over first. If it lands on time, that is a good sign for Phase 2.
  • Shared amenities. Ask which clubhouse and pools belong to your phase, and when they open.
  • Building work. If Phase 1 owners move in early, Phase 2 work will continue next door.

Adarsh has posted a Tropica master plan. Ask the sales team to mark both phases on it. Our master plan notes track what Adarsh has confirmed.

Layouts: 2.5 BHK vs 3 BHK

Adarsh Developers lists two unit types for Phase 2.

Type Super built-up (per Adarsh) Carpet area
2.5 BHK 1,385–1,395 sq ft Confirm on K-RERA
3 BHK 1,600–1,610 sq ft Confirm on K-RERA

Super built-up area includes walls, lobbies and shared space. You pay for it, but you can't use it inside your flat. So compare carpet areas when you look at rivals.

The 3 BHK is only about 215 sq ft larger than the 2.5 BHK on paper. That is our math on the smallest units. So the "third bedroom" in the 3 BHK may feel compact. Ask for room sizes on the plan.

A 2.5 BHK means two bedrooms plus a smaller room. Many buyers use it as a study. Check whether it has a window and whether a bed fits.

Ask how many flats share each floor and how many lifts serve them. Adarsh has not published those figures. Compare the layouts with the bigger flats in our Adarsh Parkland Phase 2 review.

Traffic reality

Plan for heavy peak traffic on Sarjapur Road and the ORR. Any drive time a seller quotes is a best case.

Bengaluru ranked as the world's second most congested city in a global traffic index for 2025. The average speed was 16.6 km/h, per Deccan Herald. At that speed, even a short commute can take a while at 9 am.

The Silk Board–KR Puram stretch of the ORR also needs work. Deccan Herald reported in January 2026 that the state cleared a ₹450 crore upgrade of this 17 km stretch. Road work there may slow your drive north.

How your commute may work

We have not timed these routes. Here is the route logic instead.

  • To offices along Sarjapur Road: a short run to the main road. This is Tropica's best commute.
  • To Bellandur and the ORR tech parks: you head north towards the ORR. Expect slow traffic near it.
  • To Electronic City: plan for a long ride and test it first.
  • To Whitefield: you cross the ORR and head north-east. Peak hours will hurt.

If your office is near Panathur, see our Panathur area guide.

Metro timeline

No metro serves Tropica today. The planned Sarjapur–Hebbal line, called Phase 3A, would run from Sarjapur to Hebbal.

The Karnataka cabinet cleared Phase 3A on 6 December 2024 at ₹28,405 Cr, per Deccan Herald. The 36.59 km line has 28 stations. It then went to the Union cabinet, since the Centre holds half of Namma Metro.

Deccan Herald said building would take about five and a half years after approval. Check the latest status on the BMRCL site. Don't pay a premium for a line that may be years off.

The ORR Blue Line is closer. BMRCL planned trials on the Silk Board–KR Puram line from October 2026, per Deccan Herald. It targets March 2027 for the opening.

Our investment notes will track both lines.

Risks to watch until hand-over

Buying off-plan means you carry risk for years. Keep an eye on these.

  1. RERA record. Confirm the Phase 2 number early on our RERA checklist. Keep a saved copy of the portal page.
  2. Construction pace. Ask for regular progress photos and check the quarterly updates on K-RERA.
  3. Date changes. A builder can seek an extension. Watch for any change to the filed date.
  4. Metro timing. Phase 3A still needs Union cabinet approval. Don't count on it before you sell or rent.
  5. Road work. ORR upgrades may slow your drive north for a while.
  6. Supply. Rising unsold stock in the city could hold back resale prices near hand-over.
  7. Rates. Your EMI will track interest rates over the next few years. Leave room in your budget.

Who should buy, and who should skip

Buy if you:

  • Work on Sarjapur Road.
  • Want a 2.5 BHK with a study room.
  • Can wait for an under-construction flat and pay in stages.

Skip if you:

  • Commute daily to Whitefield or Electronic City.
  • Need a metro stop within walking distance soon.
  • Want a large third bedroom. Check the plan first.
  • Need to move in within a year. Look at ready and new options.
