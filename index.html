Adarsh projects by location

North Bangalore

Devanahalli

Devanahalli sits next to Kempegowda International Airport. Adarsh sells gated villa plots here through Adarsh Savana. Buyers come for land near the airport corridor and a long holding period.

South-East Bangalore

Sarjapur Road

Sarjapur Road links the Outer Ring Road tech belt to Sarjapur town. Adarsh builds several large gated communities here, including Adarsh Tropica and Adarsh Welkin Park.

South-East Bangalore

Bellandur

Bellandur sits on the Outer Ring Road, close to RMZ Ecospace and the Cessna and Prestige tech parks. Adarsh Rosewood is the current Adarsh launch here.

East Bangalore

Panathur

Panathur Main Road runs between the Outer Ring Road and Varthur. Adarsh Parkland sits here, a short drive from Kadubeesanahalli and the Bellandur tech parks.
