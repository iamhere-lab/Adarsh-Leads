Adarsh Parkland Phase 2 at a glance

Adarsh Parkland Phase 2 is a new launch of 2.5 and 3 BHK flats on Panathur Main Road, East Bangalore. Adarsh Developers lists sizes of 1,475 and 1,850 sq ft. It has not published a price or a handover date, so ask the sales desk and check K-RERA.

Phase 2 sits next to Phase 1 of the same project. Adarsh says a bridge will link the two phases. Its brochure puts the whole campus at over 21 acres, with 70% open space.

The plan suits two kinds of buyers:

  • IT staff who work on the Outer Ring Road (ORR) or in Whitefield
  • Families who want a big 3 BHK and can wait for handover

It also suits buyers who want a known brand. Adarsh Developers says it has built in Bangalore since 1988. Its own location map marks several of its projects in East Bangalore.

It does not suit you if you need a home to live in this year. For that, look at other Adarsh flats in Bangalore.

Read our full Adarsh Parkland Phase 2 review for a deeper take. This page sticks to the facts, the costs and the risks.

Adarsh Parkland Phase 2 price list

Adarsh Developers has not published a Phase 2 price list. Third-party pages quote a wide spread of figures, and none of them is official. So we list the price as "on request" until the sales desk issues a cost sheet.

When you get a quote, check these points:

  • The base rate per sq ft, in writing
  • What "all-inclusive" covers, line by line
  • Car park, club, legal and power charges
  • The booking amount and the payment stages

What you pay on top

Karnataka charges 5% stamp duty and a 2% registration fee, plus about 0.6% cess. Deccan Herald reported the fee rise from 1% to 2% on 31 August 2025.

Add 5% GST, since the flat is still under work, per the GST Council rule published by PIB. On every ₹1 Cr of value, these taxes come to about ₹12.6 lakh. Our review's cost breakdown shows the full table.

Is Adarsh Parkland Phase 2 a good investment?

Panathur sits between Bellandur and Varthur, close to the ORR tech parks. That makes it a practical base for ORR staff.

Growth drivers to watch:

  • The ORR Blue Line, which is due by March 2027
  • A ₹450 crore upgrade of the 17 km Silk Board–KR Puram ORR stretch, cleared in January 2026 per Deccan Herald
  • Office demand along the ORR

Risks cut the other way. You pay now and move in only at handover. Your money earns no rent in the meantime.

City supply is also rising. Business Standard reported that Bengaluru's unsold stock rose 34% a year to about 79,180 homes by June 2026. Prices rose 8% over the same year.

Who should buy here

Buy here if you plan to live in the flat and hold it for years. The long wait hurts short-term flips. End users who work on the ORR gain the most.
