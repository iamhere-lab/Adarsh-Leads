Adarsh Welkin Park at a glance

Adarsh Welkin Park is a large township off Sarjapur Road with flats and villas. Adarsh Developers lists 2.5 and 3 BHK flats from 1,377 to 1,897 sq ft. It has not published a price or a hand-over date. Ask the sales desk for both.

The site sits on Rayasandra Main Road, near Gattahalli Lake. Adarsh says 72% of the land is open space. It does not publish the land area or unit count, so read them on K-RERA.

Welkin Park has three parts:

  • Sky Residences: high-rise flats in towers of G + 25 floors
  • Villas: homes on 2,400 and 4,000 sq ft sites
  • Shared campus: 75+ amenities and a 73,000 sq ft clubhouse, per Adarsh

It suits families who want a big, self-contained campus. It suits villa buyers who want a gated address near Sarjapur Road. It does not suit you if you need to move in this year.

Extra costs

Karnataka charges 5% stamp duty and a 2% registration fee, plus about 0.6% cess. Deccan Herald reported the fee rose from 1% on 31 August 2025. That totals about 7.6%, or ₹7.6 lakh for every ₹1 Cr of value.

Amenities at Adarsh Welkin Park

Adarsh lists 75+ amenities and a 73,000 sq ft clubhouse. We group the named ones below.

  • Sports: tennis, basketball and volleyball courts, a football field, box cricket, 8 badminton courts, 2 squash courts, mini golf and a skate plaza
  • Leisure: 2 banquet halls, a home theatre, a restaurant, a bar and an amphitheatre
  • Kids: a kids' pool, a trampoline park, a rock-climbing wall and a creche
  • Wellness: a gym, a health club, indoor and outdoor pools, a lap pool, a jacuzzi and a Miyawaki forest
  • Convenience: a supermarket, an ATM, a salon and a pet park
  • Safety: CCTV, controlled entry, fire systems, a sewage plant and rainwater harvesting

Ask which amenities serve flats and which serve villas.

What to check on your visit

  • Which amenities are built today and which come later
  • Whether villa owners get a separate club
  • How far your tower sits from the main gate
  • Where work on later phases will run once you move in

A long list sounds good on paper. What counts is what opens on the day you get your keys. Ask for the dates in writing. Compare the list with amenities at Adarsh Parkland Phase 2.
