Adarsh Rosewood at a glance

Adarsh Rosewood is a small project of 2 and 3 BHK flats in Bellandur, from Adarsh Developers. Flats run from 1,340 to 1,900 sq ft. The brochure lists 118 units on G + 4 floors. Adarsh has not published prices.

The sales lounge is on Doddakannelli Road, in Phase 3 of Adarsh Palm Retreat. Adarsh's website places the project next to RMZ Ecospace.

The project suits you if:

  • You work at Ecoworld, Embassy Tech Village or nearby on the ORR
  • You want a low-rise block with few neighbours
  • You can pay a Bellandur price for a short trip to work

It does not suit you if you want a big campus with a large club. Compare other Adarsh flats in Bangalore for that.

Our Adarsh Rosewood Bellandur review gives our full view. This page covers the facts, costs and risks.

Extra costs

Deccan Herald reports 5% stamp duty, a 2% registration fee and about 0.6% in cess in Karnataka. The registration fee rose from 1% on 31 August 2025. GST on an under-construction flat is 5% without input tax credit, per PIB. Together, that is about ₹12.6 lakh per ₹1 Cr of agreement value.

Ask the sales desk about car park, club, deposit and maintenance charges too.

Legal and approvals

Adarsh lists the K-RERA number as PRM/KA/RERA/1251/446/PR/311224/007335. Look it up on the K-RERA portal.

Check these items on the portal:

  1. The completion date filed with RERA
  2. The land area, units and blocks
  3. The approved plan and carpet areas
  4. Any complaints

What RERA gives you

The RERA Act makes the builder keep 70% of buyer money in a separate bank account. That money can go only to land and building costs for this project. So note the RERA date, not the brochure date.

We have not checked court records. Ask for the title deed, the khata and the encumbrance record. Our guide to A-khata vs B-khata vs E-khata explains why the khata matters for resale and loans.
