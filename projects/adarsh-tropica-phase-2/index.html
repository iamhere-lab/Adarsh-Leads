Adarsh Tropica Phase 2 at a glance

Adarsh Tropica Phase 2 price list

Adarsh Developers has not published a Phase 2 price. Third-party sites quote figures that do not agree. So ask the Adarsh sales desk for a signed cost sheet.

Extra costs

Taxes add a fixed share on top of the price you agree:

  • GST: 5% on an under-construction flat, with no input tax credit, per PIB
  • Stamp duty: 5%, per Deccan Herald
  • Registration fee: 2%, up from 1% on 31 August 2025, per Deccan Herald
  • Other duties and cess: about 0.6%

Together, that is about ₹12.6 lakh for every ₹1 Cr of agreement value. Car park, club and power charges often sit outside the base price.

Is Adarsh Tropica Phase 2 a good investment?

Tropica suits end users first. We have no rent data for it, and no published price. So we cannot give a yield or a rate per sq ft.

City data from Business Standard for Q2 2026:

  • About 15,285 homes sold in Bengaluru, up 1% a year.
  • About 21,670 homes launched, up 41% a year.
  • About 79,180 homes unsold, up 34% a year.
  • Average prices up 8% a year.

Launches are outpacing sales. That can slow price gains near hand-over. The metro could lift values later, but not soon.

The main risk

You pay in stages for years before you move in. You earn no rent in that time. We will add a rental yield once we find an official or news source for this stretch.
